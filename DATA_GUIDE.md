# Data guide: Blood Bank Inventory Balancer

All data is synthetic, generated by `generate_bloodbank_data.py` (seeded, so re-running gives the same files).

**Generator:** `generate_bloodbank_data.py` (4 blood banks, 1 Oct 2025 – 30 Sep 2026, unit-level simulation)

**Workflow simulated**

```
Whole-blood donation (daily + large camps)
  → Testing (reactive / processing losses discarded)
  → Separation into PRBC, FFP, platelets (limited capacity, prepared to a stock target), cryo
  → Storage (PRBC 35 days, platelets 5 days, FFP and cryo 365 days)
  → Issue oldest-first against daily demand (with compatibility fallbacks)
  → Unused returns (restocked if in time, discarded if late)
  → Expired units discarded
```

| Bank | Role in the story |
| --- | --- |
| Bank A (tertiary hospital) | Oncology-heavy; high platelet demand; short of PRBC and of platelets in dengue season |
| Bank B (standalone centre) | Collects more than it uses; red cells expire |
| Bank C (district hospital) | Small separation capacity |
| Bank D (private hospital) | Roughly balanced |

### `bb_daily.csv` — bank × date × component × blood group

| Column | Meaning |
| --- | --- |
| opening_stock | Units available after expiry removal and new arrivals |
| received | Units released from testing that day |
| demand, issued, unmet_demand | Units requested, supplied, and not available |
| returned_restocked | Issued units returned in time |
| discarded_expired, discarded_return, discarded_testing | Discards by reason |
| closing_stock | Units left at day end |
| near_expiry_units | Units expiring within 2 days (platelets), 7 days (PRBC), 30 days (FFP/cryo) |

### `bb_units.csv` — one row per unit

`unit_id, bank, component, blood_group, collection_date, expiry_date, outcome (Issued / Discarded / In stock),
outcome_date, discard_reason`. Includes a 30-day warm-up before 1 Oct 2025.

### `bb_donations.csv` — bank × date

`whole_blood_collected, camp_day, donations_reactive`

### Patterns built in

- Platelets have the highest discard rate (~12.8%), about 70% of it from expiry. One Indian analysis
  reported platelet discards of 8.27% with expiry causing 64.1%.
- Platelet demand peaks in Aug–Oct (dengue season); banks plan to their usual demand, so shortages appear
  at the peak. This blind spot is what a demand forecast fixes.
- Bank A runs short of PRBC (~5% unmet) while Bank B lets surplus PRBC expire → transfer opportunity.
- Donation camps (~84 camp days across banks) create gluts that later expire.
- FFP builds up (1-year shelf life, low demand). In reality, surplus plasma is often sent for
  fractionation; treat FFP mainly as context.

### Simplifications (say these openly)

- Compatibility rules are simplified (PRBC → group O same Rh; FFP → AB; platelets → any group when short).
- Demand is random daily requests by blood group, not linked to individual patients.
- Blood group shares and bank sizes are approximate and illustrative.
- No inter-bank transfers are simulated: estimating their benefit is the product's job.
